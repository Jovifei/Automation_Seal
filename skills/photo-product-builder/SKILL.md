# Photo Product Builder

## Inputs

- User-owned or commercially licensed RAW/photos.
- Target Lightroom/Camera Raw versions and tested camera profiles.
- Location, privacy, and publication decisions.

## Workflow

1. Register every source photo, RAW, preset, font, music, map, and graphic with ownership evidence.
2. Preserve master originals outside the public package; work on copies.
3. Remove or intentionally retain EXIF/GPS/identity metadata according to the product plan.
4. Develop presets as starting points, not one-click guarantees.
5. Test across multiple images, lighting conditions, cameras, and white-balance ranges.
6. Record adjustment assumptions and manual correction steps.
7. Create watermarked previews and reproducible tutorial steps.
8. Validate archive structure, install instructions, compatibility, and asset licenses.

## Outputs

- Preset files.
- Rights-cleared practice files.
- Before/after previews.
- Installation and compatibility guide.
- Location guide with safety and access caveats.
- Tutorial script/video plan.
- Rights inventory and review candidate.

## Prohibited content

Do not include third-party preset collections, unauthorized RAW files, unlicensed fonts/music, private location data, faces without suitable permission, or claims that every photo will match the preview.
