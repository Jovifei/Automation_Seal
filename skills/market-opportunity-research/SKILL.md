# Market Opportunity Research

## Preconditions

- Use public sources or user-provided material only.
- Do not scrape login-required pages or bypass rate limits.
- Define the product line, geography, audience, and time window.

## Required evidence fields

For every material claim record source title, URL, publisher/owner, publication or observation date, access date, claim supported, license/access note, and confidence.

## Workflow

1. Restate the decision the research must support.
2. Separate market size, user problem, competitor offer, price signal, and channel evidence.
3. Collect at least three independent source types when available: official platform/project data, observable listings/content, and user interviews or direct demand signals.
4. Deduplicate reposts and distinguish platform-wide numbers from the target category.
5. Record current workaround, urgency, frequency, failure cost, free alternatives, switching friction, and willingness-to-pay evidence.
6. Build a competitor sample with title, deliverables, price, proof, complaints, rights risk, and last observation date.
7. State direct evidence, inference, unknowns, and a low-cost validation experiment.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Never fabricate category GMV, conversion rate, or sales volume.
- Do not infer paid demand from views alone.
- Do not copy competitor assets into products.
- Do not treat an open repository as commercially redistributable without license review.

## Outputs

- `reports/research/<topic>/source_registry.csv`
- `reports/research/<topic>/market_report.md`
- `reports/research/<topic>/competitor_sample.csv`
- `reports/research/<topic>/assumptions_and_experiments.md`

## Quality gate

Every recommendation must identify evidence, uncertainty, expected value, validation cost, success threshold, and stop rule.
